BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026

Post date: September 29, 2026

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the notification for TRE 4.0 School Teacher recruitment. There are 32,388 vacancies. Applications opened on 25 September 2026 and close on 26 October 2026. The minimum age is 18 to 21 years depending on the class level, and the maximum is 42 years as on 01 August 2026. Please read all the details below before you apply.

Vacancy details: education qualification

Post nameEligibility criteria
School Teacher (Class 1 to 5)Bachelor's degree with 50% marks and B.Ed, or bachelor's degree with a Diploma in Elementary Education, or 10+2 with 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education, or 10+2 with 50% marks and a 4-year B.El.Ed, or master's degree with 55% marks and 3-year B.Ed-M.Ed. CTET Paper I or BTET Paper I must be qualified.
Middle School Teacher (Class 6 to 8)Graduate with a 2-year D.El.Ed, or graduate or postgraduate with 50% marks and B.Ed, or graduate with 45% marks and B.Ed (NCTE norms), or graduate with 50% marks and BA.Ed or B.Sc.Ed, or postgraduate with 55% marks and 3-year B.Ed-M.Ed. See the official notification for more.
School Teacher (Class 6 to 10)Graduate or postgraduate in the related subject with at least 50% marks and B.Ed, or 45% marks (2002 norms) and B.Ed, or 4-year BA.Ed / B.Sc.Ed. STET Paper I must be passed.
Secondary School Teacher, TGT (Class 9 to 10)Graduate or postgraduate in the related subject with at least 50% marks and B.Ed, or 45% marks (2002 norms) and B.Ed, or 4-year BA.Ed / B.Sc.Ed. STET Paper I must be passed (subject wise).
Secondary School Teacher, TGT Special (Class 9 to 10)Same qualification as TGT (Class 9 to 10), subject wise, with STET Paper I passed.
10+2 School Teacher, PGT (Class 11 to 12)Postgraduate in the related subject with at least 50% marks and B.Ed, or 45% marks (2002 norms) and B.Ed, or 4-year BA.Ed / B.Sc.Ed, or master's degree with 55% marks and 3-year B.Ed-M.Ed. STET Paper II must be passed.
